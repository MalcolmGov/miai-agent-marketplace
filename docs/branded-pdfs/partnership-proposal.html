01 Why this is worth $200k

Shopify didn’t win by selling “a few themes.” It won by owning the marketplace + commerce operating system. Agents is the same bet for MyInstantAI: a first-party surface where every customer can browse, configure, connect Actions, embed, and meter prepaid tokens — with a deep catalogue already localised for five regions. Competitors shipping a chatbot wrapper are months behind this stack.

First to market

Ship a branded agent marketplace while peers are still prototyping. Category ownership compounds; delay costs mindshare and deal cycles.

Depth × breadth

100 families × 5 markets = 500 packages — ~298K lines of authored catalogue IP (prompts, knowledge, tools, guardrails, evals) plus the runtime — not a thin demo set.

Security & trust

OIDC + workspace RBAC, audit isolation, AES-256-GCM OAuth tokens, HMAC embed keys, CSP, DSAR export — Trust Center with honest Live / Partial / Planned tags.

Regional compliance

Market packs encode US CCPA/TCPA, EU GDPR, Africa POPIA-style (incl. ZA), Asia PDPA-style, Oceania AU/NZ privacy — not one global prompt pretending to be local.

Evals as quality IP

8,500+ behavioural eval cases (~15 / agent) — regression harness for grounded answers, confirm-before-write, and cross-tenant refusals.

Build vs buy

~398K total lines already written (~298K catalogue + ~59K platform). Rebuild estimate ~$0.75–0.95M · ~50–56 months — not a weekend clone.

06 Proposed terms

Term Proposed position
Initial term 12 months from production go-live, auto-renewing for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives 60 days’ written notice.
License fee $200,000 / year, invoiced annually in advance on the go-live date. Covers the platform + full 500-agent catalogue (100 families × 5 markets).
Retainer (4 FTE) $30,000 / month, invoiced monthly in arrears (net 30). Dedicated pod: 2× Customer ops / onboarding (activate/configure, training, go-live checklists, office hours, ticket triage) · 2× Engineering (integrations — OAuth/Actions, webhook/MCP, Install, cutover onto MIAI rails — plus bugs, maintenance, workflows, new/deepened agents, evals & go-live certify). Fair-use: 12 tenants / mo + training; 10 agent-factory credits / month (typically 6–10 SKUs / improvements). Excess scoped separately.
Revenue share End-customer agents are free (no rental share). Token top-ups 20% MD / 80% MIAI — net Agents-attributed prepaid revenue after refunds and chargebacks.
Reporting & remittance MyInstantAI reports Agents-attributed token revenue monthly; Moove Digital’s share is remitted within 30 days of month-end. Either party may audit once per year.
Exclusivity Non-exclusive. MyInstantAI receives the first-to-market advantage; Moove Digital retains the right to license the catalogue to other partners.
Intellectual property Moove Digital licenses the agent packages & presets and retains ownership of all reusable IP. On termination, MyInstantAI keeps a license to agents already deployed to live customers for a 90-day wind-down.
Data protection MyInstantAI is controller and customer of record; Moove Digital acts as processor under a DPA. Data residency follows the Azure tenancy per region (US / EU / Africa / Asia / Oceania).
Currency & taxes All amounts in USD, exclusive of any applicable taxes or withholding.
