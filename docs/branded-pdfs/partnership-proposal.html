01 Why this is worth $200k

Shopify didn’t win by selling “a few themes.” It won by owning the marketplace + commerce operating system. Agents is the same bet for MyInstantAI: a first-party surface where every customer can browse, configure, connect Actions, embed, and meter prepaid tokens — with a deep catalogue already localised for four regions. Competitors shipping a chatbot wrapper are months behind this stack.

First to market

Ship a branded agent marketplace while peers are still prototyping. Category ownership compounds; delay costs mindshare and deal cycles.

Depth × breadth

92 families × 5 markets = 460 catalogue-ready packages (~2.5M chars prompts/knowledge, 936 tools) — not a thin demo set.

Security & trust

OIDC + workspace RBAC, audit isolation, AES-256-GCM OAuth tokens, HMAC embed keys, CSP, DSAR export — Trust Center with honest Live / Partial / Planned tags.

Regional compliance

Market packs encode US CCPA/TCPA, EU GDPR, Africa POPIA-style, Asia PDPA-style guardrails — not one global prompt pretending to be local.

Evals as quality IP

~3,566 eval cases (~16 / agent) — regression harness for grounded answers, confirm-before-write, and cross-tenant refusals.

Build vs buy

Rebuilding platform + catalogue in-house is years of specialised work. At ~$435 / agent / year, the license is a fraction of one senior team.

Commercial intent: $200k annual platform license (full 460) · $30k / mo support & new agents · rev-share on end-customer rental (50/50) and tokens (20% MD / 80% MIAI). What remains is agreement + cutover — not invention.
