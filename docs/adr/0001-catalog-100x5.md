# ADR 0001: Catalogue 100 × 5 markets (500 SKUs)

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-02

## Context

The marketplace sells **agent families** (vertical jobs) expanded by **market packs** (US, EU, Africa, Asia, Oceania). Early imports included a South Africa (ZA) footprint with **unprefixed** agent ids (`{family}.agent.json`). Later we standardised on prefixed ids (`us-`, `eu-`, `africa-`, etc.) and merged ZA into the Africa pack for commercial messaging.

We needed a single indexed catalogue shape for search, family cards, pricing, and eval gates — without breaking existing deep links or disk files from the ZA era.

## Decision

1. **Indexed catalogue = 100 families × 5 markets = 500 SKUs** in `data/catalog/index.json` and `families.json`.
2. The five sellable markets are **US, EU, Africa, Asia, Oceania**. ZA is **not** a sixth market.
3. **ZA unprefixed packs remain on disk** as legacy aliases (`data/catalog/{family}.agent.json`). They are **not deleted** and are **not** counted as a separate indexed market.
4. In `families.json`, each family's `markets.za` entry **points at the corresponding `africa-{family}` indexed id** (canonical Africa SKU). API `?market=za` filters resolve through that mapping for backward compatibility.
5. Regenerate `families.json` / `index.json` via scripts (`pnpm generate:packs`, `pnpm merge:za-africa`, `pnpm catalogue:ship`) — never hand-edit the index.

## Consequences

**Positive**

- Stable buyer story: 100 products, five regional packs, 500 rentable agents.
- Old ZA URLs and file paths keep working; Africa is the commercial pack.
- Eval and readiness gates target exactly 500 indexed variants.

**Negative / follow-ups**

- Disk holds more than 500 `.agent.json` files (legacy ZA aliases). Operators must understand aliases vs indexed SKUs.
- Documentation and sales must not claim a separate ZA SKU or six markets.
- Import/generator scripts must continue to skip destructive deletion of unprefixed files.
