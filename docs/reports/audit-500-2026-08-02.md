## 6. ⚠️ Do NOT delete the unprefixed files (they are ZA packs)

A prior draft recommended removing the 51 unprefixed `data/catalog/*.agent.json` files as "orphans." **That was wrong.** They are the `"za"` (South Africa) market variant:

- `families.json` → `markets.za` points at the unprefixed id (e.g. `"za": "building-management"`); 51 families have `hasZa: true`.
- `apps/web/src/lib/monday-pilot.ts` references these ids (Go-live family list).
- `scripts/certify-golive.mjs` checks them.

Deleting them would remove ZA localization and break `certify:golive`. If ZA consolidation is ever desired, it must go through the `merge:za-africa` path deliberately — not a bulk delete. **Left in place.**
